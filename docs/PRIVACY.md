Moodify is a wellbeing tracker for people aged 16 and over in the EU/EEA. It is not medical or emergency care.

## Data we process

We process account details, nickname/avatar choices, consent versions, settings, mood entries and notes, habit activity, manually entered daily health summaries, optional read-only daily step and sleep aggregates, and AI chat messages. We also process limited security, diagnostics, and privacy-respecting usage events. We do not need raw HealthKit or Health Connect samples and the app is designed to store daily aggregates only.

## Why we process it

We use this data to provide the service, sync an account, display trends and recommendations, deliver chosen reminders, secure the service, answer support requests, and meet legal obligations. [The controller must specify the lawful basis for each purpose before release.] Mood and health information may be special-category data under EU law; the production consent and lawful-basis design must be legally reviewed.

## AI chat

Chat text is sent through our Firebase backend to OpenAI to produce a response and perform safety moderation. Do not include identifying information. Chat is not therapy, diagnosis, or emergency assistance. Chat records are configured to expire after 30 days and can be cleared immediately in settings. We do not send mood or health history unless a user writes it in chat.

## Health permissions

Health access is optional and read-only. Permission can be denied or revoked in device settings. Manual values remain marked as manual. Moodify does not write to HealthKit or Health Connect in this version.

## Sharing and international transfers

We use service providers for hosting, authentication, security, diagnostics, and AI processing. [List production processors, locations, transfer mechanisms, and links to processor terms before release.] We do not sell personal data or use wellbeing content for targeted advertising.

## Retention and control

Account data is retained while the account is active, subject to the final retention schedule. Users can export data, clear AI history, and permanently delete the account in the app. Backup and security-log deletion periods must be documented before launch.

EU/EEA users may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, port, or object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a supervisory authority. Contact [privacy email] to exercise rights.

## Children, security, and changes

Moodify blocks account creation for people under 16 and does not provide a guardian-consent flow. We use access controls, server validation, App Check, and least-privilege rules, but no system is perfectly secure. Material notice changes will be communicated in the app and policy versions recorded.
